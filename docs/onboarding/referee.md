# Referee and head referee guide

## Who you are

You are a **referee**. You work the field. When you see something about a team, you record a **flag**.
Judges read the flags for the teams they interview. Your flags are a record of what an official saw.

The **head referee** heads the field. A head referee is a referee who can also set up the day and fix or remove flags.
This page covers both. Head referee tasks are marked.

## How to sign in

1. Open the app's front page. Choose the **Referee** door.
2. Type your code. The JA gives it to you. The head referee has a different code from the referees.
3. Type your **full name**. It is required. Every flag you file carries your name.
4. Press **Sign in**.

Your code does not work? Ask the JA.

## What your screen shows

You have three tabs at the bottom.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Report** | The form to record a flag. The current match and field are pinned at the top. |
| **All teams** | Every team, to scroll and pick when you cannot read a number. |
| **Today** | The teams flagged today, newest first. |

You see every team in every division. You do not see judging notes, scores, or the queue.

## Common tasks

**Start of the day (the head referee does this)**

If nobody has set up today, referees see **Waiting for the head referee**. The screen opens by itself when setup is done.

1. Head referee: choose the **Program** (VIQRC or V5RC).
2. Choose the **Number of fields**.
3. Press **Start refereeing**. This applies to all referees.

Setup shows again on each new day.

**Set the match**

1. Press **Set match** (or **Change**) on the bar at the top.
2. Choose the match type, the match number and the field. Press **Use this match**.

The match stays set for every flag you file until you change it. Change it when the next match starts.

**Record a flag**

1. Type the **Team to flag**. Or use the **All teams** tab and pick the team.
2. Under **What happened**, tap the rules that were broken. **All rules** shows the full list.
3. Under **How serious?**, tap one: Good conduct, Warning, Minor violation, Major violation or Disqualified.
4. Add a short **note** if you want to.
5. After you pick rules, you can switch on **Match affecting**. It is for Minor and Major only.
6. Press the **Record** button at the bottom. It shows the severity and team, for example Record Minor.

Minor, Major and Disqualified need at least one rule. The line above the button tells you what is missing.
If the team already has a flag for the same rule, the app shows a **Repeated** warning. It does not choose the severity for you. You decide.

**Check what is already on a team**

When you enter a team number, flags already on that team show under the form. The **Today** tab lists everything flagged today.

**Head referee: correct a flag**

1. Find the flag on the team (under the form) or on **Today**.
2. Press **Edit**. Change the severity, rules, match, field or note. Press **Save correction**.

You cannot change the team or who filed it. To fix that, remove the flag and ask a referee to file a new one.

**Head referee: remove a flag**

Press **Remove** on the flag. The app asks first. Every correction and removal is logged with your name.

**Head referee: change the setup during the day**

Press the program and fields tag on the bar at the top. Change it, then press **Start refereeing**. The app asks first, because other referees are using it.

## What you can't do, and who to ask

| You can't | Ask |
|---|---|
| Edit or remove a flag (referee) | The head referee or the JA |
| Set the program or number of fields (referee) | The head referee or the JA |
| See judging notes or scores | Nobody. They are private to judges and the JA |
| Put a team in the queue or change a booking | The queue desk |

Who can read flags: all referees, the JA, and judges (only for teams on their own panel). Teams and the queue desk cannot.
