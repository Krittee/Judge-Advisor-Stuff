# Teams and the Live board

## Who you are

You are a **team** at the event, or you are watching the **Live board**.
Teams do not sign in. There is no code. The Live board is open to everyone.

## How to open your page

**Your team's page**

1. Open the app's front page. Choose the **Team** door.
2. Type your team number, for example 1234A. Letters can be upper or lower case.
3. Open your page. It updates by itself every few seconds.

**The Live board**

On the front page, choose **Live board**. On the TV in the judges' room, the JA opens it and presses **Fullscreen**.

## What your team page shows

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A big sentence | Your status. See the table below. |
| A time | When your slot is, or how long ago you asked, or when your interview started. |
| Your panel | Your judge panel's name, its division and the judges' names. |
| **Interview in** | The language your interview will use, for example EN or TH. |
| A track | The steps: Booked, Requested, On the way, Interviewing, Complete. It shows where you are. |
| **Earlier today** | Your earlier requests from today. |

| Status | What the page says | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Booked | Your slot is booked | Grey |
| Waiting | Judges have been notified | Orange |
| On the way | Judges are on their way to you | Blue |
| Interviewing | Interview in progress | Violet |
| Complete | Interview complete — thank you! | Green |

If you have no request, the page says **No request yet**.

## What the Live board shows

- At the top: how many teams are **waiting for a judge**, how many are **in interview**, and how many are **complete**.
- One column for each judge panel, with a tile for each team. A tile shows the team number, its status, a time, and the team name.
- Waiting teams have the biggest, orange tiles, so judges see them first.
- **What the colours mean** is a key on the board. The colours match the table above. A team that is not in the queue yet has a plain tile.
- **By panel** and **Pit floor** switch the view. Pit floor shows where each team's pit is.
- **Hide completed** removes finished teams from the screen.

The board shows no judging notes, scores or flags.

## Common tasks

**Ask for an interview now**

Go to the queue desk. They put you in the queue. Your page turns orange.

**Book an interview for later**

Go to the queue desk. Choose a time. Your page shows Booked and the time.

**Tell the staff which language you want**

Tell a coordinator in the pits. They save it. It then shows on your page.

**Find your team on the board**

Look in your panel's column. Your tile changes colour as the interview moves on.

## What you can't do, and who to ask

| You can't | Ask |
|---|---|
| Request a judge or book a time yourself on the page | The queue desk or a coordinator |
| Cancel or change a booking | The queue desk |
| Set your interview language | A coordinator or the JA |
| See notes, scores or referee flags | Nobody. They are private |
| Fix a wrong or missing team number ("Check the number, or ask the Judge Advisor to add you") | The Judge Advisor |
| Get a panel, if the page says **No judge panel assigned yet** | The Judge Advisor |
