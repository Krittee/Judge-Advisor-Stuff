# Queue desk guide

## Who you are

You work the queue desk. Teams come to you and ask for an interview.
You put the team in the queue, or you book it a time. You can also undo a mistake or move a booking, until judges answer.

## How to sign in

1. Open the app's front page. Choose the **Queue desk** door.
2. Type the queue desk code. The JA gives it to you. Everyone at the desk uses the same code.
3. Type your name if you want. It is optional. The JA sees it in the activity log.
4. Press **Sign in**.

Your code does not work? Ask the JA.

## What your screen shows

At the top, two buttons choose what you are doing:

| Button | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Now** | The team is ready now. It goes straight into the queue. |
| **Book** | The team wants a time later. You pick a slot. |

Below them:

- A **Team number** box. Type the number. The screen shows the team's name, notebook type, pit, panel, and how busy that panel is.
- The team's **interview language**. You can see it. You cannot change it.
- In **Now**: the list **Waiting now**. It shows teams that are waiting or that judges are going to.
- In **Book**: the list **Booked later**, and the panel's time grid.
- **All teams** at the bottom. Tap a team to select it.

## Common tasks

**Put a team in the queue now**

1. Choose **Now**.
2. Type the team number.
3. Add a note for the judges if you want to.
4. Press the language button, for example **Request · English**. The team turns orange on the Live board.

The team's own language button is first. A team that can use both languages gets one button for each.

**Book a time**

1. Choose **Book**.
2. Type the team number.
3. Choose the language. Add a note if you want to.
4. Tap an open slot. Press **Book** with the time.

Slots that are taken or too close to another booking cannot be picked. Judges need 20 minutes to walk between interviews.

**Undo a mistake**

In **Now**, find the team under **Waiting now** and press **Undo**. This only works while the status is Waiting, before a judge answers.

**Move a booking to a new time**

1. Press **Reschedule** (next to the team, or in **Booked later**).
2. Tap a new open slot. Press **Move to** that time.

The language and the note stay as they were.

**Cancel a booking**

While moving a booking, press **Cancel booking**. The app asks first.

**A team with a booking is ready now**

Type the team number. Press **Request now instead**. Their old slot opens up for someone else.

## What you can't do, and who to ask

| You can't | Ask |
|---|---|
| Add a team that is not in the app | The JA |
| Queue a team with no panel yet | The JA assigns a panel |
| Add a team that is already in the queue | Nobody. A team can be in the queue only once |
| Change a request after judges said On the way | The JA |
| Change a team's interview language | A coordinator or the JA |
| Move an interview forward, or read judging notes and scores | Judges. These are private to them and the JA |
| See referee flags | Nobody at the desk. The JA and judges see them |
| Move a team to another panel | The JA |
