# Coordinator guide

## Who you are

You are a coordinator. You walk the pits and talk to teams.
You ask each team which language it wants for its interview, and you save the answer.
You can also add a team to the queue, or book it a time. You cannot change a booking that already exists.

## How to sign in

1. Open the app's front page. Choose the **Coordinator** door.
2. Type the coordinator code. The JA gives it to you. All coordinators use the same code.
3. Type your **full name**. It is required. Every booking you make carries your name.
4. Press **Sign in**.

Your code does not work? Ask the JA.

## What your screen shows

You have two tabs at the bottom.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Teams** | Every team, in a list. Each row has the team number, name, division, pit, its status, and language buttons (such as **EN** and **TH**). |
| **Slots** | One panel's time slots for today. Open slots have a **Book** button. |

On the Teams tab you can search by team number or name, pick a division, and turn on **Not asked** to show only teams with no language yet.
The line under the filters counts the teams, how many are asked, and how many are not asked yet.

You do not see judging notes, scores, team messages, conflicts or referee flags.

## Common tasks

**Save a team's interview language**

1. Find the team on the **Teams** tab.
2. Tap the language button: **EN**, **TH**, or both. It saves at once.
3. To remove a language, tap its button again. A team with no language shows **Ask**.

Do this first. You cannot request or book a team until it has a language.

**Add a team to the queue now**

1. Tap the team's row to open it.
2. Press **Request**, then the language, for example **Request · English**.

The team turns orange on the Live board. A team can only be in the queue once.

**Book a time for a team**

1. Tap the team's row to open it. Press **Book a time**. The **Slots** tab opens on that team's panel.
2. Check the bar at the bottom. It says **Booking for** and the team number.
3. Press **Book** next to an open time. It shows **Picked**.
4. Press the **Book** button in the bar at the bottom.

Times that are taken show the team number. A time marked **Too close** is too near another booking, because judges need 20 minutes to walk between interviews.

## What you can't do, and who to ask

| You can't | Ask |
|---|---|
| Change, move or cancel a booking or a request that already exists | The queue desk. The screen says: "To change it, ask the queue desk." |
| Write a note for the judges | The queue desk |
| Request or book a team with no panel | The JA assigns a panel |
| Add a team that is not on the list | The JA |
| Move a team to another panel | The JA |
| Read judging notes, scores, team messages or referee flags | Nobody. They are private |
