# Judge guide

## Who you are

You are a judge. You interview teams on **one panel**.
You see that panel's teams. You move each interview along, take notes, score the teams, and nominate teams for awards.
The Judge Advisor (JA) runs the event and decides the awards.

## How to sign in

1. Open the app's front page. Choose the **Judge** door.
2. Type your **panel code**. The JA gives it to you. Everyone on your panel uses the same code.
3. Type your name if you want. It is optional. The JA sees it in the activity log.
4. Press **Sign in**.

You are signed out at the end of the day. If the JA deletes your panel, you are signed out too.
Your code does not work? Ask the JA. Do not keep guessing.

## What your screen shows

You have three tabs at the bottom.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Queue** | The team to see next, big at the top. Below it: **Waiting**, **With us**, **Booked**, **Not in queue**, and **Done**. |
| **Scores** | Your panel's teams ranked on points. |
| **Today** | Your panel's time slots for today, with the teams booked in them. |

On the big card you see the team's number and name, its notebook type, its interview language, its pit, and how long it has waited.
If a referee flagged the team, you see the flag. If the queue desk wrote a note, you see it in quotes.

You only see teams in your panel's division. A team your panel has a conflict with does not appear at all.

## Common tasks

**Interview a team**

1. Look at the big card at the top. It shows a team you are already with. If there is none, it shows the team that has waited longest. If nobody waits, it shows the next booked team.
2. Press **On our way**. The team shows blue on the Live board.
3. When you are with the team, press **Start interview**. The team shows violet and a timer starts.
4. When you finish, press **Finish interview**. The team shows green.

You can only move one step at a time. Pressed the wrong step? Ask the JA to reset it.

**Score a team**

1. Press **Open team sheet** (or **Notes & scores**).
2. Choose **Notebook** or **Interview** at the top.
3. For each line, tap a score. Every tap saves at once. Tap the same score again to clear it.
4. To clear a whole sheet, press **Clear Notebook scores** or **Clear Interview scores**. The app asks first.

If a team has an Ungraded notebook, its Notebook sheet is not counted. The top of the team sheet says so.

**Write a note**

1. Open the team sheet and choose **Notes**.
2. Type in the box. Press **Save note**.

Notes and scores are private to judges and the JA. Referees, the desk and teams never see them.

**Nominate a team for an award**

1. After you finish an interview, a green card asks **Nominate (team number) for an award?**
2. Tap the awards you want. Every tap saves. Tap again to remove it.
3. Press **Add a reason** to tell the JA why. Press **Done** when finished.
4. Later, open the **Done** list and use **Edit** or **Nominate** on that team.

The JA decides the winners. You never see who won.

**Read a referee flag**

Flags show on the team's card and at the top of the team sheet. They say what the referee saw and who reported it. You can read them. You cannot change them.

## What you can't do, and who to ask

| You can't | Ask |
|---|---|
| Work with a team on another panel | The JA |
| Cancel a request, or change a booking | The queue desk (early only), or the JA |
| Move a team to another panel | The JA |
| Skip a step or go back a step | The JA |
| Report that you know a team (a conflict of interest) in the app | Tell the JA. The JA records it, and the team leaves your panel |
| File, correct or remove a referee flag | A referee, or the head referee |
| Change a team's interview language | A coordinator, or the JA |
| Decide or see award winners | The JA |

If you know a team personally, tell the JA **before** you interview them.
